# Domperidone: From Molecule to Market to Monitoring

## Integrated Regulatory Affairs, Pharmacovigilance & Data Analysis Case Study

**Portfolio Project by Swastika Prasad Mohanty**  
**B.Pharm | Regulatory Affairs | Pharmacovigilance | Data Analysis**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This is a self-directed portfolio project exploring the journey of **domperidone** from regulatory authorization and safety restrictions to pharmacovigilance signal analysis and risk assessment.

The project integrates three areas:

- Regulatory Affairs
- Pharmacovigilance
- Data Analysis

The objective is to demonstrate the ability to interpret publicly available regulatory and pharmacovigilance information and connect it into a structured pharmaceutical case study.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Review the regulatory history and safety-related actions concerning domperidone.
- Understand how regulatory safety concerns can lead to restrictions and risk-minimisation measures.
- Analyse published pharmacovigilance data related to cardiac events.
- Apply a structured approach to signal identification.
- Develop a mock PSUR-style safety assessment.
- Demonstrate basic rule-based risk scoring using selected risk factors.
- Present pharmaceutical information in a structured and transparent manner.

---

## 🏛️ Regulatory Affairs Component

The regulatory section reviews the development of safety restrictions surrounding domperidone, including:

- Historical regulatory actions
- Cardiac safety concerns
- The 2014 European regulatory review
- Dose and duration restrictions
- Subsequent regulatory actions
- Comparison of selected regulatory approaches

A CTD-style Module 2.5 extract is also included to demonstrate structured clinical/regulatory writing.

---

## 🛡️ Pharmacovigilance Component

The pharmacovigilance section examines published safety data and uses it to demonstrate a simplified signal-assessment workflow.

The project considers:

- Cardiac adverse events
- Concomitant QT-interacting medicines
- Severity patterns
- Biological plausibility
- Signal identification and escalation considerations

A mock PSUR-style section is included to demonstrate safety-data interpretation and structured PV writing.

---

## 📊 Data Analysis Component

The project uses published aggregate pharmacovigilance data rather than a raw safety database extract.

The analysis includes:

- Event-frequency calculations
- Percentage calculations
- Comparative interpretation of severity groups
- Risk-factor identification
- Rule-based risk scoring

The risk-scoring approach uses illustrative factors such as:

- Age above 60 years
- Dose above 30 mg/day
- QT-interacting medication
- CYP3A4 inhibitor use
- Pre-existing cardiac history

Each factor is assigned one point for an illustrative score.

**Important:** This is a transparent rule-based approach and is **not a trained machine-learning model**.

---

## 🔍 Key Data Insight

The project analyses published FAERS-based information and other publicly available safety information.

One limitation is that the available published data do not provide a controlled background comparison for all relevant exposure groups. Therefore, the analysis does **not** attempt to calculate a true relative risk from these figures.

This distinction is important when interpreting pharmacovigilance data.
